What is it?

Pizza Tower is a 2D platformer made by Tour De Pizza and released in January 2023. It takes its speed and slapstick spirit from the Wario Land series. You play Peppino Spaghetti, a nervous chef whose pizzeria is threatened by a giant floating pizza, Pizzaface.

Each level is a sprint: explore, rack up points, then escape before the timer runs out.

How it plays

  • Mach run

    Hold a direction to build speed through four gears. Faster means more power and more risk.

  • Combos

    Keep hitting enemies and collecting toppings to build a combo before it times out.

  • Escape

    Grab the exit trigger and the level flips into a countdown. Run back before it hits zero.

  • Taunt

    Peppino can taunt at any time. It's silly, and it's also part of the scoring and secrets.

Who's who

Peppino Spaghetti

The hero. Anxious, expressive, and surprisingly fast.

Gustavo & Brick

A gnome chef and his pet rat. Together they have a playstyle of their own.

The Noise

Peppino's chaotic rival, with a different set of moves.

Pizzaface

The big, grinning threat at the top of it all.

Rank your run

Every level ends with a grade based on your score. Reach the top for an S, then go for the P rank, which asks for more than points.
