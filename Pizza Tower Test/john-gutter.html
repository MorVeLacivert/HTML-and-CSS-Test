Choose difficulty

Click the game once so it picks up your keys.

How to play

Controls

  • ←→ or AD move
  • Space jump
  • Hold Shift to run
  • T taunt
  • N music on/off

Mach run

Hold Shift and a direction to build speed. At full Mach you smash block walls and Forknights.

The escape

Touch the pillar at the far end of the huge level to start the countdown, then race back to the exit door. Every finished lap makes the timer tick faster.

Ranks

Collect toppings for a D to S rank. For a P rank, get everything and finish all the laps.

Power-ups

Ghost potion

Turn half see-through and walk through walls and floating platforms for 10 seconds.

Speed potion

Walk faster and reach top Mach speed almost instantly for 12 seconds.

Bird wings

Jump again in mid-air, as many times as you like, for 15 seconds.

Difficulties

Easy

3:30 countdown. Two laps for a P rank. The timer speeds up slightly after each lap.

Medium

6:00 countdown. Three laps in one escape for a P rank. The timer speeds up normally after each lap.

Hard

12:00 countdown. Four laps in one escape for a P rank. The timer speeds up a lot after each lap.
